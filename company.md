# About

It started with a laptop and a deadline.

My first projects were freelance jobs, built late at night in Kathmandu, figuring things out one bug at a time. That habit of owning a problem from start to finish has stuck with me.

Over 3 years later, I've worked with software companies in Lalitpur and with clients in Nepal and Australia. The work has included offline field apps used across all 77 districts of Nepal, community apps on Google Play and the App Store, and dashboards that teams use every day.

I work mainly with React, React Native, and Expo, with TypeScript and Node.js behind them. I didn't just pick these tools up. I enjoy working with them, and I've built my whole workflow around them.

I work alone, on purpose. When you hire me, you talk to the person writing your code. No account managers, no hand-offs. I keep things simple: write clean code, communicate openly, own the work fully, and make each project better than the last.

Kathmandu is where I work. Everywhere else is where I deliver.
